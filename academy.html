MASTERY ENGINE

Don't just get the answer. Prove you understand it.

Four short modes turn reading into usable knowledge: diagnose a misconception, retrieve the idea, apply it to a new situation, then explain it in your own words.

01 · DIAGNOSE

Catch the trap

“Low P/E means cheap.” “Profit means cash.” “More debt always means more return.” Identify the hidden assumption before it becomes a research mistake.

02 · RETRIEVE

Recall without looking

Come back later and answer from memory. The system can increase or reduce difficulty based on your previous attempt.

03 · APPLY

Transfer the idea

A concept you understand in one example can fail in another. Apply the same reasoning to a changed fictional situation.

04 · TEACH BACK

Explain it simply

If you cannot explain the concept without jargon, you probably do not own the mental model yet.

PRACTICE

SOURCE LITERACY

Learn to separate a number from the evidence behind it.

A research system is only as strong as its evidence trail. Practise identifying what is verified, calculated, assumed and still unknown.

VERIFIEDDirectly supported by an identified primary or authoritative source.
CALCULATEDDerived transparently from verified inputs using a reproducible calculation.
ASSUMPTIONA chosen estimate, scenario or interpretation that must remain visible.
UNKNOWNImportant information that has not yet been verified. Never silently fill the gap.
PROTECT YOURSELF

Financial knowledge includes knowing when information is trying to manipulate you.

Learn the mechanics of fraud, pressure tactics, misleading claims and information asymmetry before encountering them in the real world.
