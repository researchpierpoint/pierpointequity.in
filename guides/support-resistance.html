Support and Resistance Explained

Understand price zones, breakouts, retests and false breakouts.

Support and resistance describe areas where buying or selling interest has appeared historically.

Zones

Treat levels as areas rather than exact prices.

Breakouts

Volume and sustained trading beyond a zone can add context, but false breakouts occur.

Use

Combine levels with trend, liquidity and broader market conditions.
