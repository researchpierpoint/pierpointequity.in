Portfolio Rebalancing Explained

Understand why portfolios drift and how investors can restore intended exposures.

Price movements can change portfolio weights even when no new money is invested.

Why rebalance

It can restore a chosen risk allocation or reduce accidental concentration.

Methods

New contributions, partial sales or scheduled reviews can be used depending on circumstances and taxes.

No universal frequency

The appropriate review process depends on the portfolio and objectives.
