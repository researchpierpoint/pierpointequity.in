Investing vs Trading Explained

Understand the difference between long-term investing, swing trading and short-term trading.

The two approaches differ in time horizon, research focus, risk management and decision rules.

Investing

Long-term investors generally focus on business economics, earnings, cash flow, competitive position and valuation.

Trading

Trading focuses more on price behaviour, liquidity, catalysts, timing and predefined risk limits.

Choose the framework

Do not mix a long-term thesis with a short-term trade without explicitly defining the time horizon and invalidation.
