Risk-Reward Ratio Explained

Understand expected upside versus defined downside without treating the ratio as a guaranteed outcome.

Risk-reward compares a planned loss with a planned gain under stated assumptions.

Scenario

Define entry, invalidation and target assumptions before calculating the ratio.

Probability

A high ratio can still lose if the scenario is unlikely.

Portfolio context

Position size and correlation matter as much as the ratio.
