Futures Trading Explained

Understand futures contracts, margin, expiry, mark-to-market and leverage.

Futures create contractual obligations under exchange rules and can provide large exposure relative to margin.

Margin

You generally post margin rather than paying the full notional value, creating leverage.

Mark-to-market

Daily price movements can create cash obligations and gains/losses.

Risk

Small underlying moves can create large percentage changes relative to the margin posted.
