SIP Explained for Indian Investors

Understand systematic investment plans, instalments, rupee-cost averaging and what SIP does not guarantee.

A SIP is a method of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals in an eligible mutual-fund scheme.

How it works

Regular contributions buy units at the applicable NAV. More units are bought when NAV is lower and fewer when it is higher.

No guarantee

SIP does not guarantee profits or remove market risk. It is a contribution method.

Choose the fund first

Evaluate objective, risk, costs, benchmark and portfolio instead of selecting a fund simply because SIP is available.
