Candlestick Charts Explained

Learn open, high, low, close and the most common candlestick concepts.

A candlestick compresses four price observations into a visual representation for a chosen period.

Read first

Understand body, upper wick, lower wick, open and close before learning named patterns.

Context

Patterns are less meaningful without trend, support/resistance, volume and timeframe.

Avoid certainty

No candlestick pattern guarantees a future move.
