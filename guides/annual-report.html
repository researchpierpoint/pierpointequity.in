How to Read an Annual Report

A practical order for reading an Indian company's annual report without getting lost in hundreds of pages.

Annual reports contain business, financial, governance and risk information. The goal is to find material facts efficiently.

Start here

Read the business overview, management discussion, consolidated financial statements, cash flows, debt notes and auditor report.

Notes matter

Accounting policies, related parties, contingent liabilities, segment reporting and exceptional items can materially change interpretation.

Cross-check

Compare management claims with actual revenue, margins, cash flow, capital expenditure and balance-sheet trends.

Keep evidence

Record the page, period and source date for material facts so later updates do not mix different information sets.
