Trading Volume Explained

Understand volume, turnover and why a volume spike needs context.

Volume measures the quantity of securities traded; turnover measures traded value under the relevant market definition.

Confirmation

Price moves accompanied by unusual volume can provide additional context.

No guarantee

Volume does not tell you whether buyers or sellers are “winning” by itself.

Use

Compare volume with a security's own history and liquidity profile.
