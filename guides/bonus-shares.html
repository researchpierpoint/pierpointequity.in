Bonus Shares Explained

Understand bonus shares, share-count adjustment, price adjustment and what a bonus does not change.

A bonus issue increases the number of shares held by eligible investors under specified terms.

Economic effect

The number of shares rises and the quoted price adjusts; the bonus itself does not create economic value.

Check terms

Read the official announcement for ratio, record date, ex-date and eligibility.
