FII/FPI and DII Buying Explained

Understand foreign and domestic institutional flows and what daily flow data can and cannot tell you.

Institutional flow data describes aggregate activity by defined investor groups.

Use

Flows can explain market liquidity and positioning, especially over longer periods.

Limit

Aggregate buying does not identify which companies were bought or whether an individual stock is attractive.

Context

Combine flow data with earnings, valuation and macro conditions.
