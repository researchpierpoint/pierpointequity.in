Profit Growth and PAT Growth Explained

Understand PAT growth, CAGR, consistency, one-off gains and the difference between accounting growth and durable growth.

Profit growth is useful only when you understand what caused it. Compare PAT with revenue, margins, cash flow and share count.

CAGR

CAGR smooths a multi-year change into an annualised rate. It does not show the path between the start and end points.

Quality of growth

Separate operating improvement from tax changes, exceptional items, other income, asset sales or unusually weak prior-year profits.

EPS versus PAT

EPS can grow faster or slower than PAT because the share count changes. Examine both.

Stress test

Ask what happens if margins normalise, demand slows or a temporary benefit disappears.
