How to Research Potential Multibagger Stocks

A framework for researching companies that could compound for years without treating a multibagger outcome as predictable.

Study business growth, reinvestment opportunity, competitive advantage, execution and valuation.

Opportunity

Look for a credible large market, room for expansion and attractive incremental returns on capital.

Valuation

A strong business can still produce poor returns when the starting valuation embeds excessive expectations.

Failure cases

Write down what would invalidate the thesis: slower growth, margin pressure, dilution, leverage, competition or governance issues.
