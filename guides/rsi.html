RSI Explained

Understand the Relative Strength Index, extreme readings and divergence.

RSI is a momentum oscillator commonly displayed on a 0–100 scale.

Extremes

High or low readings can indicate strong momentum, but trends can remain extreme for long periods.

Divergence

Price and RSI moving differently can be a research signal, not a guaranteed reversal.

Context

Use RSI with price structure, trend, volume and a defined risk plan.
