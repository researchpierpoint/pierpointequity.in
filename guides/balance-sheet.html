How to Read a Balance Sheet

Learn assets, liabilities, equity, debt, cash, working capital and what a balance sheet says about financial strength.

The balance sheet is a snapshot of what a company owns, owes and the residual equity attributable to shareholders.

Assets

Separate cash, receivables, inventory, fixed assets and intangible assets. Understand which assets generate revenue.

Liabilities

Review debt, payables, provisions and other obligations. Check maturities and interest where disclosed.

Working capital

Track receivables, inventory and payables against revenue and cost trends. Working-capital expansion can absorb cash.

Balance-sheet quality

Look for rising leverage, weak liquidity, large unexplained assets, aggressive capitalisation or rapid changes in working capital.
