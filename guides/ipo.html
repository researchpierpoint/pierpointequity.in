How to analyse an IPO before buying

An IPO prospectus contains the business model, financial statements, risks, use of proceeds, ownership and offering details. Start there rather than with launch-day excitement.

Fresh issue vs offer for sale

Fresh shares raise money for the company; an offer for sale transfers existing shares from selling shareholders. The economic implications are different.

Valuation

Compare the proposed valuation with listed peers while adjusting for growth, margins, returns, leverage and business quality. Avoid using a peer multiple without checking whether the economics are comparable.

Use of proceeds

Understand exactly where fresh capital is intended to go. Debt repayment, capacity expansion, acquisitions and general corporate purposes create different future outcomes.

IPO checklist

  • ☐ Prospectus read
  • ☐ Fresh issue vs OFS understood
  • ☐ Use of proceeds checked
  • ☐ Peer valuation normalised
  • ☐ Risks and related parties reviewed
