Liquidity Explained

Understand trading volume, market depth, spreads and market impact.

Liquidity describes how readily an asset can be transacted without materially moving its price.

Volume

High volume can support liquidity but does not guarantee it at every price.

Depth

Displayed bids and offers provide information about available liquidity at quoted levels.

Risk

Thinly traded securities can be difficult to exit when conditions deteriorate.
