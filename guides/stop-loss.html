Stop-Loss Explained

Understand stop-loss orders, mental invalidation levels and why gaps can defeat an intended exit price.

Risk exits can be implemented in different ways depending on market and order type.

Level

A stop level should connect to the reason the position is being held, not an arbitrary percentage alone.

Gap risk

A security can move through a stop level, especially during news or illiquid trading.

Position size

Risk management starts before entry; smaller positions can reduce the impact of an adverse move.
