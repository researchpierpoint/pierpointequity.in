Moving Averages Explained

Understand simple and exponential moving averages and how traders use them.

A moving average smooths price data over a selected window.

SMA vs EMA

SMA weights observations equally within the window; EMA gives greater weight to recent observations.

Uses

Trend identification, dynamic reference levels and crossovers are common uses.

Limitation

Moving averages are lagging transformations of price data.
