How to Read an Earnings Call

Learn how to separate reported results, management commentary, guidance and investor questions.

An earnings call contains management commentary and analyst/investor questions around a reporting period.

Start with results

Read the reported financial statements before accepting narrative explanations.

Listen for changes

Watch for changes in demand, pricing, margins, capacity, capex, working capital and guidance.

Challenge

Compare commentary with later reported outcomes rather than treating management statements as facts.
