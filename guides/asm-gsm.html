ASM and GSM Stocks Explained

Understand Additional Surveillance Measure and Graded Surveillance Measure concepts and how they can affect trading conditions.

Exchange surveillance frameworks can impose additional conditions on securities meeting specified criteria.

What it means

Measures can affect trading conditions, margins, price bands or other mechanics depending on the applicable stage.

Not a valuation verdict

A surveillance measure is a market-control mechanism and is not by itself proof of fundamental quality or weakness.

Verify

Check the latest exchange notice because surveillance status can change.
