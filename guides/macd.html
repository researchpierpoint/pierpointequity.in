MACD Explained

Understand MACD, signal lines, histogram and momentum interpretation.

MACD is a momentum indicator built from moving averages.

Components

MACD line, signal line and histogram describe relationships between moving averages.

Use

Crossovers and divergence can provide context, especially alongside trend and volume.

Limitation

Indicators do not remove uncertainty or guarantee reversals.
