Promoter Holding Explained

Understand promoter shareholding, changes over time and why promoter ownership needs governance context.

Promoter holding describes shares held by promoters under applicable disclosure definitions. It is one part of ownership analysis.

Trend

Track promoter holding across quarters and investigate large increases or decreases.

Pledge

Promoter pledging can create additional risk because pledged shares may be sold if obligations are not met. Check the latest disclosed data.

Not a quality score

High promoter ownership is not automatically positive, and low ownership is not automatically negative. Examine governance, capital allocation and institutional participation too.

Dilution

New equity issuance can reduce an existing promoter percentage even if the promoter does not sell shares.
