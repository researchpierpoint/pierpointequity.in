THE QUESTION LIBRARY

Start with the question.
Not the jargon.

Most people do not arrive knowing whether they need a P/E ratio, a balance sheet or an ETF guide. They arrive with a question. PirePoint starts there.

I’m completely newWhat could go wrong?How do I research?How do I learn another market?
01 · STARTING

“I don't know where to begin.”

The questions that turn uncertainty into a usable foundation.

How do I start investing?Start with the market, ownership, orders and the basic mechanics.Learn the foundation → What actually is a stock?Understand what ownership means before looking at prices.Understand shares → How do I actually buy a stock?Understand the path from account to order to settlement.See the process → What is the difference between a broker and an exchange?Separate the infrastructure from the service you use to access it.Learn the distinction → When is the market open?Learn why market hours, sessions and holidays matter.Understand market hours → What happens after I buy?Follow the trade through execution, settlement and ownership.Understand settlement →
02 · RESEARCH

“How do I know what I'm looking at?”

Move from a price on a screen to an actual research process.

How do I research a company?Business → financial engine → quality → valuation → expectations → risks.Follow the full process → What should I actually check?Build a repeatable fundamental-analysis framework instead of collecting random ratios.Build the framework → How do I read financial statements?Connect earnings, assets, liabilities, working capital and cash.Read the statements → Why is profit not the same as cash?See how working capital, capex and accounting choices change the picture.Understand cash flow → How do I read an earnings call?Separate useful evidence from narrative and promotional language.Read an earnings call → Which numbers actually matter?Learn why the right metric depends on the business model and question.Build the model →
03 · VALUE & EXPECTATIONS

“Is the price reasonable?”

A number is not an answer until you understand what it means and what the price already assumes.

What does P/E actually tell me?Learn the ratio, its context, its limitations and when it can mislead.Understand P/E → Is a high ROE always good?Separate genuine operating quality from leverage and accounting effects.Understand ROE → What does ROCE tell me?Connect returns to the capital required to produce them.Understand ROCE → How can I value a business?Explore multiples, DCF and the assumptions hidden inside valuation.Learn valuation → Is today's valuation high or low?Use historical context without treating the past as a guaranteed future.Study historical valuation → What does the current price require?Reverse the valuation and ask what growth, margins and reinvestment are implied.Study expectations →
04 · RISK & PROTECTION

“What could go wrong?”

People often ask for returns first. Good research also asks what can break the thesis, the portfolio or the process.

How much should I put into one idea?Learn position sizing, concentration and the difference between conviction and exposure.Learn position sizing → How diversified should I be?Understand what diversification can and cannot protect you from.Study diversification → How do I think about risk versus reward?Turn an idea into explicit upside, downside and uncertainty.Learn risk-reward → Why do investors make the same mistakes?Understand behavioural biases before they become expensive habits.Study investor behaviour → How do I recognize a market scam?Learn common fraud patterns, warning signs and verification habits.Protect yourself → What is a stop-loss really for?Understand its mechanics, trade-offs and why it is not a magic safety switch.Understand stop-losses →
05 · FUNDS & ETFS

“Should I use a fund instead?”

Funds look simple from the outside. The important questions are what they own, how they track, what they cost and what risks remain.

What is an ETF?Understand structure, trading, diversification, costs and the difference between market price and NAV.Learn ETFs → How does a SIP work?Understand the mechanism without confusing regular investing with a guaranteed outcome.Learn SIPs → How does a mutual fund work?Understand pooling, NAV, management, costs and what investors actually own.Learn mutual funds → Why can an ETF price differ from NAV?Understand premiums, discounts, liquidity and the mechanics behind exchange trading.Understand ETF pricing →
06 · THE WORLD

“I know one market. How do I learn another?”

Do not restart from zero. Learn the destination market's differences.

How do I invest across countries?Learn the framework for currency, access, regulation, taxation, market structure and research differences.Learn global investing → I know India. How do I learn China?Use the market bridge to learn what changes rather than relearning universal concepts.Open the China guide → I know another market. How do I learn the US?Build a destination-market model before comparing securities.Open the US guide → What changes when I cross a border?Think about access, currency, rules, reporting, tax and market-specific risk.Build a market bridge →
07 · REAL-LIFE QUESTIONS

“What do I do with all of this?”

Financial education becomes useful when it answers decisions people actually face.

How do dividends work?Understand distributions, dates, yield and what a dividend does—and does not—mean.Learn dividends → How are stock gains taxed?Learn the concepts, categories and why tax rules must be checked for the relevant market and date.Understand capital gains → What happens when a company buys back shares?Understand buybacks, ownership effects and the questions to ask.Study buybacks → What does a stock split actually change?Separate the share count from the economic value of the ownership.Understand splits →
THE PIREPOINT RULE

Every answer should create a better next question.

When you finish, you should know what you understand, what you still do not know, and where to go next.
