Every important lesson should answer seven questions.

01 · What is it?Definition is only the start.
02 · Why?Connect it to a real problem.
03 · How?Understand the mechanism.
04 · Test itUse evidence or calculation.
05 · What fools me?Study failure modes.
06 · TransferSee what changes elsewhere.
07 · RetrieveExplain it without looking.
