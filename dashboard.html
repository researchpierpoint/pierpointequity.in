THE PIREPOINT LOOP

Learning becomes useful when it survives outside the page.

01 · LearnBuild the mental model and vocabulary.
02 · RetrieveClose the page and reconstruct the idea.
03 · ApplyUse it on a new fictional situation.
04 · VerifyFind the source and separate fact from interpretation.
05 · Red-teamAsk what could make your explanation fail.
06 · TransferTest what changes when the market changes.
MASTERY, NOT PAGE VIEWS

Three levels of proof.

RecallCan you explain the concept without looking at the page?
ApplicationCan you use it on a new example rather than repeat the definition?
TransferCan you recognise how the same concept changes in another market?
