🇺🇸 COUNTRY GUIDE

United States stock market

The US market is a deep, highly liquid equity market with extensive public-company disclosure and several major exchanges and venues.

REGULATOR

SEC

Market supervision and investor-protection framework.

EXCHANGE

NYSE / Nasdaq

Primary listed-market infrastructure and major market venues.

CORE IDEA

Know the rules before the stock

Learn how US listed companies disclose information, how exchanges work, how dividends and splits operate, and how individual investors access shares.

Common terms

S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, earnings call, ADR, market order, limit order.

Research habit
