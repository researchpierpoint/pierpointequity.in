INVESTMENT TOOLS

Useful tools. Nothing unnecessary.

Five high-use calculators for everyday investing. Fast, transparent and built around Indian investors.

Stock Average Price

Find your new average price after adding more shares.

Weighted average of the existing and new purchase cost.

Compound Interest

See how money grows when returns compound over time.

Mathematical illustration only; the return rate is an input, not a forecast.

CAGR Calculator

Measure annualised growth for an investment or an insurance policy.

Insurance Policy CAGR compares total premiums paid with maturity value. Because premiums are paid over time, XIRR is more precise when payment dates are available.

SIP Calculator

Estimate the future value of regular monthly investing.

Illustrative future value using monthly compounding; actual returns vary.

Lumpsum Calculator

Estimate how a one-time investment could grow over time.

Illustrative future value only; the return rate is an assumption.

How these tools work

Stock average uses weighted purchase cost. CAGR = (final value ÷ initial value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1. Compound interest applies the selected compounding frequency. SIP models equal monthly contributions. Lumpsum applies annual compounding to a single investment.

These calculators provide mathematical outputs, not personalised investment advice, forecasts or guarantees.
