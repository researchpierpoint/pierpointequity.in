HOW THE ROUTINE WORKS

A route that changes as your evidence changes.

Adaptive learning works best when the system tracks the underlying skill rather than treating one quiz percentage as the learner model. PirePoint applies that principle to market education.

1 · Discover

Start with your goal, prior exposure and destination market.

2 · Calibrate

Use short cases to test applied understanding and confidence.

3 · Build

Connect prerequisites, concepts, practice and research tasks.

4 · Adapt

Review weak or forgotten skills instead of forcing a fixed course order.
