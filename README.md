# PirePoint Equity

PirePoint Equity is a **Stock Market Guide** built around durable understanding rather than live market-data competition.

## Product

- Common stock-market guides
- Country-by-country market guides
- Searchable market glossary
- Cross-country market comparison
- Transparent learning calculators
- Official-source links for rules that can change

## Product principle

**Explain first. Sell nothing.**

PirePoint intentionally does not maintain a large live stock, mutual-fund or ETF data terminal. The goal is to answer the questions investors keep needing after today's price has disappeared from the screen.

## Quality

Guide pages are checked automatically for required metadata, internal links and core hub pages through the Guide Quality GitHub Action.

## Publishing

PirePoint is a single-editor publication. Content is intended to be published only by the site owner. The public website does not provide a community-posting, guest-posting or user-content workflow.

Educational information only; not personalised investment advice.
