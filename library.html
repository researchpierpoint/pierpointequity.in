THE DIFFERENCE

Every important lesson should answer seven questions.

01

What is it?

Definition is only the starting point.

02

Why does it matter?

Connect the idea to an actual research problem.

03

How does it work?

See the mechanism, not just the terminology.

04

How do I test it?

Use evidence, calculations, comparisons or scenarios.

05

What could fool me?

Study counterexamples and failure modes.

06

What changes in another market?

Learn the universal principle and then the local rule.

07

Can I explain it without looking?

If you cannot retrieve it, you probably have not mastered it yet.
