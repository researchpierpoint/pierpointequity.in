QUALITY BAR

What every important lesson should contain

Why this mattersA concrete reason this concept changes how someone reads markets.
Plain-English mental modelThe simplest accurate explanation before technical detail.
Worked exampleA complete example, not a definition disguised as a lesson.
Common mistakeThe shortcut or misconception most likely to mislead the learner.
PracticeA question or case where the learner must produce an answer.
Evidence checkpointWhere to verify the important fact or rule using an appropriate source.
Connection mapWhat this concept unlocks next and what it depends on.
Transfer testHow the idea behaves differently in another market, asset class or context.
THE PRINCIPLE

Teach independence, not dependence.

PirePoint should never require a learner to trust PirePoint blindly. The goal is to make the learner better at asking questions, checking evidence, recognising uncertainty and doing the research themselves.
