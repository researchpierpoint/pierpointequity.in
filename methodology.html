METHODOLOGY

How PirePoint works

Evidence first

Material factual claims are intended to be traceable to their underlying source and reporting period.

Separate facts from analysis

We distinguish sourced facts, calculations, interpretations, scenarios and unknowns.

Challenge the thesis

Research is designed to examine contradictory evidence and potential failure cases, not only supporting evidence.

Keep history

Research versions should preserve what was known at the time so later updates do not rewrite history.

Human control for high-risk actions

Automation can handle routine research operations, while potentially regulated, legal, security or other high-risk actions are designed to stop for review.
