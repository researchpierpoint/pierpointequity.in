PIREPOINT CASE SCHOOL · FICTIONAL

Don't predict the answer.
Make the research move.

Each case removes company names, headlines and tips. You receive incomplete information, choose the next research move, see the consequence, then explain what changed in your thinking.

01 · ECONOMICS

Growth that consumes cash

Revenue compounds, but receivables and inventory absorb more capital while incremental returns fall.

02 · VALUATION

The apparently cheap multiple

A valuation multiple sits well below its history. You must determine whether the price changed or the economics changed.

03 · EVIDENCE

The perfect story

A polished narrative makes several claims. Your first job is to decide what deserves verification before interpretation.

04 · ACCOUNTING

Profit without cash

Reported earnings rise while operating cash flow deteriorates. Find the bridge before forming a view.

05 · RISK

The comfortable thesis

Every important fact supports your initial idea. The task is to deliberately search for disconfirming evidence.

06 · GLOBAL

Same company, new market

A framework transfers across borders, but market structure, currency, reporting and access create new variables.
