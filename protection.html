Certainty discipline

Separate what is known, inferred, estimated, disputed and unknown. Never turn uncertainty into fake precision.

Before trusting a claim

  1. Find the original source.
  2. Check the date and definition.
  3. Check what the number actually measures.
  4. Look for missing context and alternative explanations.
  5. Ask what evidence would prove the claim wrong.
  6. Record your confidence separately from your conclusion.
