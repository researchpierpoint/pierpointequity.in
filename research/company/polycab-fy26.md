# Polycab India Limited — FY26 evidence snapshot

**Status:** factual research snapshot, not a personalised recommendation.

## Verified facts
FY26 revenue was ₹288,838 million, EBITDA ₹40,057 million and PAT ₹27,084 million. Revenue increased 29% year over year and PAT increased 32%. Company-reported RoCE was 31.5%, net cash was ₹41,940 million, debt/equity was 0.01 and FY26 capex was ₹14,799 million. The company also states it plans ₹60–80 billion of capex over five years under Project Spring. Sources are linked in the structured record.

## What changed
The FY26 result represents a substantial increase in scale versus FY25: revenue rose from ₹224,083 million to ₹288,838 million and PAT from ₹20,455 million to ₹27,084 million. The company says core wires and cables remained the primary growth engine and FMEG had consecutive profitable quarters.

## Interpretation
The evidence supports a picture of strong FY26 operating growth and a very low-leverage balance sheet. That is an interpretation of the cited facts, not a forecast.
