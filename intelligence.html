ONE COMPANY · SEVEN QUESTIONS

A research page built around how people actually investigate.

Start broad, then progressively reveal the evidence and assumptions that deserve attention.

01 · BUSINESS ENGINE

What does it actually do?

Products, customers, revenue drivers, pricing power and the economic engine behind reported growth.

Understand the business before the ticker.
02 · QUALITY

Does the economics compound?

ROCE, ROE, margins, cash conversion, reinvestment and balance-sheet resilience.

03 · FINANCIAL ENGINE

Where did the money go?

Revenue → EBIT → profit → operating cash → free cash flow, with the reason for each change.

04 · EXPECTATIONS

What does today's price require?

Separate what the business is worth from what the market price already assumes.

05 · RED TEAM

What could break the thesis?

Business, financial, valuation and governance failure modes—not a generic risk paragraph.

06 · EVIDENCE

How do we know?

Primary-source hierarchy, dates, calculations, contradictions and explicit unknowns.

07 · MEMORY

What changed since last time?

Research history, thesis changes and the evidence that caused you to change your mind.
