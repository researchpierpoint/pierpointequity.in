# Red Team protocol

Before public release, challenge every material research update:

1. What evidence could be wrong, stale or misinterpreted?
2. Is the comparison period consistent?
3. Is the source primary and identifiable?
4. Does another source materially disagree?
5. Is a calculation reproducible from stored inputs?
6. Are facts being presented as forecasts?
7. Is the language accidentally promotional, personalised or recommendation-like?
8. What would falsify the interpretation?
9. What information is still unknown?
10. Does the update need human/legal review?

A failed challenge creates a review event. It must not be silently bypassed.
