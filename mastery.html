SIX MODES

One system. Six ways to learn.

01 · LEARNBuild the mental model

Start with first principles, then move toward mechanics, financials, valuation, risk and market structure.

 02 · RETRIEVETest what you remember

Questions expose gaps that rereading can hide. Answer before seeing the explanation.

 03 · APPLYChange the assumptions

Use fictional cases to see how growth, cash flow, leverage and valuation interact.

 04 · RESEARCHWork with evidence

Turn a question into evidence, assumptions, contradictions, scenarios and a reasoned conclusion.

 05 · CONNECTCross markets intelligently

Keep universal concepts while learning what changes in another country's market, currency, rules and research environment.

 06 · AUDITChallenge your own thinking

Ask what is sourced, calculated, assumed, unknown, contradictory and capable of changing your conclusion.

RESEARCH HYGIENE

Before trusting your own conclusion.

01 Separate observation from interpretation.
02 Record assumptions instead of hiding them.
03 Prefer primary evidence where appropriate.
04 Search deliberately for contradictory evidence.
05 Ask what would change your mind.
06 Distinguish precision from false certainty.
07 Keep market-specific rules separate from universal concepts.
08 Treat an unanswered question as unanswered.
